# Task 093 — selections, masks and painting end-to-end workflow (P1)

Priority: P1. Depends on Task 090. Expected effort: 10–16 focused hours.

## Objective

Make the common select → mask/paint → transform → save workflow complete,
fast and semantically consistent for raster and vector-backed layers.

## Required work

- Audit rectangle/ellipse/row/column/lasso families and new/add/subtract/
  intersect, feather, antialias and off-canvas behavior.
- Separate raster mask, vector mask and active edit target; do not collapse two
  Photoshop masks into one slot.
- Ensure selection ants/caret/gizmos remain overlay-only and dirty no document
  composite; animate with bounded presentation submissions.
- Paint/erase/fill on raster layers created after PNG/PSD/PDF open, including
  selection coverage, transparency lock, pressure and mask targets.
- One completed brush gesture creates one undo entry at pointer-up and uses
  dirty-tile history.
- Save/reopen, merge/rasterize and PSD projection retain/report semantics.

## UI exposure

Reuse current toolbar flyouts, options-bar controls, mask thumbnails/borders and
Layers actions. Clearly distinguish multi-selected layers from active mask/layer.

## Verification and completion

- Packaged smokes for every implemented selection family and paint target.
- Overlay-only telemetry: zero composite submissions during ants/caret changes.
- Visual fixtures for feather/edges/transforms and PSD mask roundtrip.
- Stress gestures for latency, undo bytes and stable-tail GPU/heap behavior.
- Run full affected gates, update contracts, commit and move.
