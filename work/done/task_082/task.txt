# Task 082 — complete wide-gamut Photoshop blend and color roundtrip parity

Status: completed 2026-08-06. The 48-case Photoshop matrix passes at RMSE
0.07..0.79, including Adobe RGB Hard Mix 8/16-bit at 0.13/0.15. Adobe RGB
8/16-bit source samples retain their precision to the GPU decode boundary;
semantic text/vector/gradient/FX colors share the document transform; PSD
exports embed a compact CC0 sRGB ICC profile. Full quick quality, 40-case FX,
ten-template inventory and EHS-396 compositor endurance gates pass.

## Objective

Finish the remaining color-management and compositor work discovered by the
controlled Photoshop profile matrix. Keep LightTable's high-precision linear
GPU working representation, while evaluating Photoshop/PDF blend semantics in
the document's declared profile domain without mode-specific patches.

## Current measured baseline

- The original 32-case untagged 8-bit blend corpus passes.
- 46 of 48 untagged/sRGB/Adobe-RGB, 8/16-bit matrix cases are close to
  Photoshop (RMSE at or below 2.55).
- Adobe-RGB Hard Mix remains structurally different:
  - 8-bit RMSE 10.27;
  - 16-bit RMSE 47.47.
- The remaining difference occurs mainly at binary threshold boundaries after
  per-layer conversion to sRGB.
- PSD raster previews, patterns and composites are normalized lazily through
  LittleCMS, but semantic colors and true high-precision/wide-gamut pixel
  preservation are not yet covered by one complete document transform.

## Required architecture

- Keep these concepts explicit and separate:
  - source ICC/profile and source precision;
  - canonical GPU storage/processing space;
  - declared document blend profile;
  - display/export profile.
- Add one reusable document color-transform service. PSD, PDF, native save,
  semantic text/vector/style colors and the compositor must consume this
  service instead of implementing private profile conversions.
- Evaluate blend equations, opacity and coverage in the declared document
  blend profile inside the existing GPU compositor pass.
- Support common matrix/TRC profiles such as sRGB and Adobe RGB directly and
  use a cached GPU LUT generated through LittleCMS for arbitrary ICC profiles.
- Do not introduce per-mode Adobe-RGB fixes, compatibility branches, CPU
  readbacks, extra full-frame passes or texture-sized intermediates.
- Preserve out-of-sRGB and 16-bit information. Remove the current RGBA8 import
  bottleneck where it would clip or quantize authoritative wide-gamut pixels.
- Normalize PSD semantic colors as well as raster content:
  - text runs;
  - vector fills and strokes;
  - gradient stops and pattern colors;
  - Layer Style colors;
  - supported adjustment color operands.
- Make Assign Profile and Convert Profile distinct, truthful, undoable document
  operations. Keep Convert disabled until all authoritative content can be
  transformed consistently.
- Apply and embed the selected output profile in PNG/TIFF/native/PSD export as
  appropriate. Preserve enough source/profile metadata for reliable native and
  PSD roundtrip.

## Acceptance criteria

- No supported profile is silently treated as sRGB.
- Adobe-RGB Hard Mix passes the same numeric gate as the rest of the controlled
  matrix without a mode-specific exception.
- Semantic and raster colors agree after import, edit, native reopen and PSD
  roundtrip.
- 16-bit/wide-gamut source information is not clipped by an RGBA8 authoritative
  intermediate.
- Ordinary sRGB interaction and render performance do not materially regress.
- The compositor remains the single layer-evaluation authority and no new
  parallel renderer or duplicated color pipeline is introduced.
- Architecture parity documents are updated with final measurements and the
  completed task is moved from `work/todo` to `work/done`.
