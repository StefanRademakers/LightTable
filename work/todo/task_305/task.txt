LightTable Vello path decomposition and render-island architecture

Goal
Profile the complete Vello update path without attributing LightTable integration
cost to Vello itself. Remove avoidable unchanged-scene work, establish correct
visibility retention, diagnose the first-close spike, and specify the production
render-island architecture without changing canonical document editability.
