# Task 047 - PSD tight raster surfaces and load performance

Status: in progress

Photoshop raster previews must remain layer-local from worker serialization
through retained GPU storage. Expanding every layer to the document canvas makes
large layered PSDs need several gigabytes of VRAM, delays opening and produces
uninformative layer thumbnails.

## Baseline corpus

Use all matching PSD/reference-image pairs below
`D:\mediavibe\LightTableTestFiles\psd\templates\Save the Date Invitation PSD 6`.
EHS-396 currently loads 42 layers at about 4.4 seconds ready time, roughly
4.37 GB estimated GPU memory and 17.54% composite/reference difference.

## Latest verified run (2026-08-03)

All ten packaged-Electron opens completed without page errors after the tight
surface and raw transient-upload changes. Ready time ranged from 1.15 to 1.87
seconds and estimated GPU memory from 704 MB to 2.98 GB. EHS-396 improved from
about 4.39 to 1.73 seconds and 4.37 to 1.09 GB. Its rounded reference difference
moved from 17.54% through the PNG path to 17.57% from the direct ag-psd RGBA8
bytes. Across the complete corpus the direct path improved five comparisons,
was unchanged on three and shifted two by only +0.01/+0.03 percentage point.
Large existing fidelity gaps in EHS-395 (95.24%) and EHS-404 (99.29%) are
visible reconstruction/unsupported-adjustment work, not load failures; they
must not be hidden by corpus averages.

On 2026-08-04, a brush stroke on the tight imported `Join us` preview in
EHS-396 was saved to native LightTable format v4 and reopened successfully.
The layer retained its local dimensions, off-canvas-capable offsets, transform,
painted pixels and document placement. Legacy v1-v3 native files retain their
previous document-sized raster interpretation.
