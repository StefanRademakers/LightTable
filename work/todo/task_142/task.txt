Task 142 - Preserve ordered Grade and Lens FX processing layers in PSD export

Goal
====

Export LightTable's ordinary Grade Layers, Lens FX Layers and attached/local
processing honestly to PSD while preserving their exact visible tree position.
Use genuine Photoshop semantics only where measured equivalence exists and an
explicit rendered fallback everywhere else. The PSD must never apply processing
twice or imply editability that Photoshop cannot reconstruct.

Accepted product model
======================

Task 143 removed the separate Global Grade / Global Lens FX pseudo-layer model.
This task must not reintroduce it.

- A Grade Layer at the top of the root stack is global because of its position.
- A Grade Layer in a group or midstack affects only the eligible lower composite
  at that exact position.
- Lens FX Layers follow the identical visible-stack rule.
- Attached/local Grade and Lens FX belong to their drawable owner.
- Flatten and raster export already use the canonical live evaluator and are the
  appearance source of truth.

There is no document-global state outside `document.layers` to export and no
special top-level `Global Grade` folder is created merely because a processing
layer happens to be topmost.

PSD representation strategy
===========================

Grade Layer
-----------

Project each Grade Layer at its exact sibling/group position. A Grade Layer can
contain several ordered modules, so its initial editable PSD representation may
be a collapsed folder named after the LightTable layer, containing verified
Photoshop adjustment layers in the same order.

Only emit a genuine adjustment descriptor when the specific semantics are
proven sufficiently equivalent. Matching labels are not evidence. Candidate
families include Curves, Exposure, Hue/Saturation and Color Balance, but every
adapter retains its own parity and roundtrip gate.

For native LightTable controls without a faithful Photoshop equivalent—such as
the compound Light model, Lift, Point Color, Camera Raw-style B&W Mix or a
creative Look—insert an explicitly named rendered fallback at that exact layer
position or fail closed according to export intent. Never silently substitute a
visually different Photoshop adjustment.

Lens FX Layer
-------------

Project every Lens FX Layer at its exact visible position. Lens distortion,
lens blur/depth, halation, film Grain, creative chromatic aberration and
post-crop vignette do not currently have a complete reliable Smart Filter
roundtrip. Preserve appearance with an explicitly named per-layer rendered
fallback until a descriptor and Smart Object source contract are proven.

A Lens FX Layer in the middle of a group may not be moved to document output or
collapsed with another Lens FX Layer for export convenience.

Attached/local processing
-------------------------

Keep the processing attached to its owning drawable in the PSD projection.
Where Photoshop Smart Object / Smart Filter semantics are proven, map them
there. Otherwise preserve the owner plus an explicit appearance fallback and a
structured warning. Do not promote attached processing into a free adjustment
layer, because that changes scope.

Masks, opacity, blend and clipping
==================================

Test the representation rather than assuming a PSD folder behaves like a
LightTable processing layer:

- translate the processing layer's mask at the same semantic boundary;
- preserve visibility and stack order;
- prove partial opacity and supported blend modes against the lower composite;
- prove clipping behavior and group pass-through/isolation;
- if folder opacity/mask semantics differ, use a rendered per-layer fallback;
- do not use a top-level appearance snapshot to hide an incorrect editable
  projection unless the user explicitly chooses Maximum Appearance.

Export intents
==============

1. Editable PSD
   - preserve the ordinary layer/group tree;
   - emit verified Photoshop adjustment descriptors at exact positions;
   - use clearly named local rendered fallbacks for unsupported modules;
   - return structured warnings for every loss of semantic editability.

2. Maximum Appearance PSD
   - bake through the same deterministic boundary used by Flatten/raster export;
   - prioritize the exact visible result over editability;
   - never retain active processing above an already baked result;
   - never double-apply a Grade or Lens FX Layer.

An optional `LightTable Appearance` safety snapshot may be investigated, but it
must be user-selected and clearly reported because it obscures editable content
until hidden. It is not a semantic roundtrip.

Implementation requirements
===========================

- Consume the canonical ordered layer tree and compositor plan, not Properties
  panel state or a reconstructed list of global modules.
- Preserve group nesting and exact sibling positions.
- Keep PSD descriptor generation separate from GPU implementation details.
- Reuse the live final-render/bake boundary for appearance output.
- Keep the working pipeline at 16-bit/HDR precision until the selected PSD
  encoding boundary and report the writer's actual bit-depth limitation.
- Preserve current behavior for documents without processing layers.
- Keep `.lt` as the lossless editable source of truth.
- Store enough projection metadata to issue stable, inspectable compatibility
  findings on reopen and re-export.

Verification matrix
===================

For Grade Layer and Lens FX Layer cover:

- root top, middle and bottom positions;
- before/after raster, vector and text layers;
- nested isolated and pass-through groups;
- multiple processing layers in both orders;
- visibility off/on;
- neutral and extreme settings;
- absent, white, black, partial and feathered masks;
- opacity 0, partial and 100%;
- supported blend modes and clipping chains;
- attached/local processing combined with free processing layers;
- 8/16-bit inputs, alpha and wide-gamut/profile fixtures.

Open every oracle PSD in the supported current Photoshop version and verify:

- initial appearance;
- exact layer/folder position and collapsed state where a folder is used;
- adjustment editability and descriptor values;
- visibility, masks, opacity, blend and clipping;
- Photoshop's regenerated composite after editing a lower layer;
- structured warnings for every rendered or omitted semantic;
- LightTable reopen and re-export behavior.

Add projection unit tests, Photoshop roundtrip fixtures and pixel comparisons
against the canonical LightTable render. Use task_203's safe version-pinned
Photoshop automation; never capture through a conflicting instance or unsaved
user documents.

Definition of done
==================

Every Grade/Lens FX processing layer appears at its true visible stack position
in PSD. Verified modules remain editable, unsupported processing has a local and
honestly named appearance fallback, masks/scope/order are preserved or reported,
and both export intents avoid double application. Photoshop rebuilds after a
lower-layer edit without exposing a hidden global-processing discrepancy.

Implemented foundation - 18 August 2026
========================================

- Editable PSD remains fail-closed when an exact descriptor is unavailable.
- Attached Photoshop-compatible adjustments are excluded from baked raster
  pixels and emitted once as clipped editable layers; unsupported attached
  processing remains locally baked, avoiding double application in both cases.
- The product now exposes a separate `Photoshop PSD (Maximum Appearance)...`
  export. It writes exactly one `LightTable Appearance` raster from the
  canonical composite, emits no active processing descriptors and skips layer
  and LUT readback. Its `-appearance.psd` suffix makes the loss of editability
  visible outside the application.
- Projection and ag-psd roundtrip tests prove that a document containing a
  Grade Layer becomes one raster layer with no adjustment descriptor in this
  intent. Editable projection remains the default for command automation.
- A Grade Layer with only native `lt.curves` authored now projects at its exact
  tree position as a collapsed pass-through folder containing one editable
  Photoshop Curves adjustment. Its wrapper is neutral; opacity, blend mode and
  an authored raster mask are projected onto the single Curves child. The no-op
  initial white mask is omitted to avoid Photoshop group isolation. Clipping
  remains fail-closed until cross-folder clipping against an external base is
  proven in Photoshop. Active Layer Styles, unavailable mask pixels and
  unsupported modules also stop Editable export.
- PSD compatibility findings are now structured with a stable code, tree path
  and severity. Appearance-preserving losses of editability no longer abort an
  Editable export: baked attached/local processing, Face Warp, raster-backed
  text/vector fallbacks and missing Smart Object source packages are reported
  as `degraded-editability`. Missing pixels or an unproven free processing-layer
  projection remain `blocking` and stop before a file is published.
- Unsupported attached/local adjustments now emit an explicit
  `attached-adjustment-baked` finding at their owner path. Their canonical
  owner-layer readback already contains the effect, and no second PSD
  adjustment child is emitted, so the result preserves appearance without
  double application.
- Projection fixtures now prove Curves-only Grade Layers retain their exact
  sibling position between raster layers and retain ordered, independently
  hidden processing folders inside a pass-through group. This closes the
  root/midstack and nested-order unit gates for the first editable Grade
  decomposition; Photoshop rebuild evidence remains version-blocked by
  task_203.
- The packaged desktop PSD roundtrip now targets the explicit Editable menu
  intent and passes against `D:\TextTest.psd`: command export, in-memory reopen,
  semantic signature comparison and physical host save all completed without
  renderer errors. The external Photoshop rebuild remains a separate oracle
  gate rather than being inferred from this LightTable roundtrip.
- The generated interchange contract and its registry-count gate now cover all
  20 current processing modules (previously 15). The five newly inventoried
  modules are Face Warp, Grade Look, Black & White Mix, the shared Photoshop
  adjustment node and Local Contrast. Its PSD wording now matches actual
  policy: verified descriptor, appearance-safe owner bake, or a blocking
  finding—never a silent free-layer omission.
- The complete generated interchange evidence audit passes across 79 rows,
  and packaged Editable PSD roundtrips pass for both `D:\TextTest.psd` and
  `D:\shapes.psd`. These prove the current LightTable host/import/export route;
  they do not replace the blocked version-pinned Photoshop oracle.
- Successful command/API PSD exports now retain structured compatibility
  findings on their opaque artifact metadata. Automation can therefore tell a
  fully editable artifact from an appearance-correct but degraded one without
  receiving binary payloads or scraping console text. Blocking findings still
  terminate the task and publish no artifact.
- A neutral compound Grade Layer can now decompose, in evaluator order, into
  editable Curves, Photoshop-compatible Gradient Map and one native Photoshop
  adjustment child inside the existing collapsed pass-through folder. The
  projector reuses the already proven descriptor adapters and roundtrips the
  ordered children through ag-psd. If a compound layer has opacity, Fill,
  blend or an authored mask, export remains fail-closed: those boundary
  semantics cannot be moved to one child without changing the combined result.

Still open: further editable decomposition of compound Grade Layers, position-local
fallbacks for unsupported free Grade/Lens FX Layers, masks/opacity/blend/group
semantics for those projections, 16-bit writer support, and the version-pinned
Photoshop rebuild matrix after task_203 restores the Adobe oracle.

- PSD export now reports its actual encoding boundary as a structured
  `degraded-fidelity` finding. Exporting a 16- or 32-bit LightTable document
  explicitly states that the current ag-psd writer quantizes to 8 bits/channel
  only at the PSD boundary; the live compositor and `.lt` source remain at
  their authored precision. Eight-bit documents receive no such warning.
- Editable export now identifies a free Lens FX Layer with the dedicated
  blocking code `lens-fx-unprojectable` and directs the user to Maximum
  Appearance. It no longer falls through to the generic native-adjustment
  warning, which could incorrectly suggest that a normal Photoshop adjustment
  descriptor was merely missing.
